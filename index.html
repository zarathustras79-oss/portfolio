About Me (&)
My Journey

I have worked as an assistant professor, program director, newspaper editor and grant consultant.

'03

Bachelor of Arts, Harvard University

I studied Social Studies and graduated magna cum laude.

'03

Program Director, Tieng Xanh-Voice, Inc., Dorchester, Massachusetts

I wrote grants, built a mentoring curriculum and led workshops for at-risk youth from 2003 to 2005.

'06

Graduate Research Fellow, East-West Center, Honolulu, Hawaii

I analyzed national youth survey data and helped design the second national survey of Vietnamese youth.

'07

MA in Public Policy, Harvard Kennedy School

I studied Political and Economic Development.

'07

Chief Foreign Editor, Thanh Nien Daily, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

I launched the English news edition and led a newsroom of 10 foreign editors and 30 Vietnamese journalists until 2009.

'10

Education Consultant, Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education, Malden, MA

I built the review process for a $60-million School Improvement Grant program (2010 to 2011).

'10

Consultant, LIN Center for Community Development, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

I wrote the blueprint and strategic plan that secured the legal license for the LIN NGO Community Center.

'15

M.Phil. in Socio-Cultural Anthropology, Yale University

I completed my master of philosophy degree in anthropology.

'16

Vietnam Academic Director, CET Academic Programs, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

I built experiential learning programs for over 200 international students and managed the regional staff until 2018.

'21

Lecturer of Anthropology, Smith College, Northampton, MA

I taught anthropology courses and mentored Mellon Mays undergraduate scholars.

'22

Ph.D. in Socio-Cultural Anthropology, Yale University

I earned my doctorate in anthropology.

'22

Assistant Professor of Pacific-Asian Studies, University of Victoria, Victoria, Canada

I teach and mentor students on Asia-Pacific topics and secure research grants. I hold this job today.
